Research programme

Intelligent systems that remain useful when the world gets messy.

A common thread runs through my academic and applied work: how systems infer from incomplete evidence, represent uncertainty and make decisions that remain useful under changing conditions. My work has moved from human-centred perception into embodied intelligence, and now also includes reliable LLM and agentic systems.

Featured research · ARISE

Assembly and Robotics Innovation in Steel Building Erection

I am a Research Fellow at Queen's University Belfast on ARISE, an international US-Ireland collaboration spanning Queen's, New York University, the University of Galway and the University of Texas at San Antonio, supported through linked DfE, NSF and Science Foundation Ireland / Research Ireland awards.

Within Queen's contribution I lead machine-learning and computer-vision research for robotic structural assembly. Outputs include ConPose, ISC-Perception and emerging work that moves from perception towards richer embodied intelligence for contact-aware assembly.
