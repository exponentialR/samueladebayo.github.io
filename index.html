Current research · ARISE

Machine learning and computer vision for robotic structural assembly.

I am a Research Fellow at Queen's University Belfast on ARISE, a US-Ireland collaboration with New York University, the University of Galway and the University of Texas at San Antonio. I lead machine-learning and computer-vision research within the Queen's team.

The work includes ConPose, ISC-Perception and the robotic assembly strategy reported at ISARC 2025.
