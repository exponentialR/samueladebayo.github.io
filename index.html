Machine learning for perception, interaction and reliable intelligent systems.

I am Dr Samuel Adebayo, a machine-learning researcher and computer scientist. My research began with visual cues and intention inference in human-robot collaboration and now extends into robotic structural assembly. My applied work also examines evaluation and assurance for LLM and agentic systems in production.

Current research · ARISE

Machine learning and computer vision for robotic structural assembly.

I am a Research Fellow at Queen's University Belfast on ARISE, a US-Ireland collaboration with New York University, the University of Galway and the University of Texas at San Antonio. I lead machine-learning and computer-vision research within the Queen's team.

This work includes ConPose, ISC-Perception and the robotic assembly strategy reported at ISARC 2025.
