Current research · ARISE

Robotic assembly as a testbed for reliable embodied intelligence.

I am a Research Fellow at Queen's University Belfast on ARISE, a US–Ireland programme involving Queen's, New York University, the University of Galway and the University of Texas at San Antonio. Within Queen's contribution, I lead machine-learning and computer-vision research for robotic structural assembly.

The work has produced ConPose and ISC-Perception, alongside experimental tooling that connects perception, simulation and physical interaction.
